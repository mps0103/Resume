# Play Store listing copy

## App name (30 char max)
Resume Builder — CV Maker

## Short description (80 char max)
Build a resume offline and export a clean, ATS-ready PDF. No account needed.

## Full description (4000 char max)

Write your resume once. Switch designs as often as you like without retyping
anything.

Resume Builder works completely offline. There is no sign-up, no account, and
nothing you write is uploaded anywhere — your details stay on your phone.

**Built to get past the filter**
Most job applications are read by software before a person sees them. Every
export is a real text PDF, not a picture of one, so applicant tracking
systems can actually read it.

**What you get**
• Guided fields for experience, education, skills, projects, certifications
  and languages
• Add, rename, reorder or hide any section
• Live preview — see the finished page while you type
• Everything saves as you go
• Keep as many resumes as you want, tailored per application
• Export to PDF and share straight to email or WhatsApp

**Free**
Three watermarked exports and the free designs. Supported by ads.

**Pro**
Removes the watermark and the export limit, unlocks every design, and turns
off ads. Monthly or yearly, billed through Google Play, cancel any time.

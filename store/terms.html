The app

Resume Builder lets you write a resume and export it as a PDF. The free version includes a limited number of watermarked exports and shows ads. Pro removes the watermark and the export limit, unlocks every design, and turns off ads.

Subscriptions

Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Google Play. Payment is charged to your Play account at confirmation. It renews at the same price for the same period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in the Play Store under Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Google under Play's refund policy.

Your content

Everything you write stays yours. It is stored on your device and we have no access to it. You are responsible for the accuracy of what you put on your resume.

Your data is local

Because resumes are stored only on your device, we cannot recover them if you delete the app, lose the device, or clear its data. Export or back up anything you want to keep.

Acceptable use

Do not use the app to create documents that impersonate another person or misrepresent qualifications you do not hold.

The app is provided as-is. We do not guarantee that a resume made with it will result in an interview or a job.

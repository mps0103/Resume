Privacy Policy

Resume Builder · last updated 12 September 2026

What we collect

Resume Builder does not collect, transmit or store your personal information on any server. Everything you type — your name, contact details, work history and every resume you create — is saved only in the app's private storage on your own device.

What leaves your device

Two things, both handled by Google rather than by us:

  • Advertising. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to select ads. You can reset or delete this identifier in your Android settings under Privacy, Ads.
  • Subscriptions. Payments are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only whether an active subscription exists.

Google's own handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Files you export

PDFs you export are written to your device's storage and shared only when you choose to share them. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Deleting your data

Deleting a resume in the app removes it permanently. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Because nothing is held on our side, there is no account to close and no deletion request to make.

Children

Resume Builder is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

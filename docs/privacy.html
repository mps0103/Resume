Privacy Policy

Resume Builder (com.mps.resumebuilder) · Last updated 11 October 2026

In short: your resumes never leave your phone. We operate no servers and hold no copy of anything you write. The only data that leaves your device goes to Google, for ads on the free plan and for processing payments.

1. Who we are

Resume Builder is published by Dealtrix. You can reach us at info@dealtrix.com about anything in this policy.

2. What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Your resume content — name, contact details, work history, education, skills, projects, and any other section you fill in
  • Your chosen design, colours and layout settings
  • App settings and your count of free exports used
  • A local record of whether your subscription is active

None of this is transmitted to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync. The app works with no network connection at all, and that is deliberate.

Files you export

PDF and Word files you export are written to your device's Downloads folder. They stay on your device unless you share them yourself. Backup files you create from Settings are likewise yours alone.

3. What is shared with third parties

We use three Google services. We do not use any analytics product.

ServiceWhenWhat it receives
Google AdMob Free plan only. No ads are requested or shown once you subscribe. Your device's advertising ID, plus device and general usage information, used to select and measure ads. Governed by Google's own privacy policy.
Google Play Billing Only if you subscribe or restore a purchase. Handles the entire payment. We never see or store your card details — only whether your subscription is active.
Google Play Integrity Occasionally, at app start. Checks the app has not been tampered with. Returns a signed verdict about the app and device. It sends none of your resume content.

Advertising consent in the EEA and UK

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the app asks for your consent before requesting any ad, using Google's consent management platform. You can change or withdraw that consent at any time from Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices.

4. What we collect

Nothing. We run no servers, no analytics and no crash reporting of our own, and we have no way to read what you write. Crash reports, if any, reach us only through Google Play Console in aggregated form, and contain no resume content.

5. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. Ad content is limited to a general audience rating.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes every resume, setting and local record it holds. Because we keep no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and nothing we can restore.

This cuts both ways. If you lose your phone, or uninstall without a backup, your resumes are gone for good. Use Settings → Back up to a file and keep that file somewhere else.

7. Permissions

The app requests internet access (for ads and billing) and the advertising ID permission that Android requires for ads. It does not request storage, contacts, camera, location or microphone access.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date at the top.

9. Contact

Questions, corrections or requests: info@dealtrix.com.
